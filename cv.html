Work Experience

High School Mathematics Tutor: Over the past 6 years, I have taught mathematics to students across various age groups and topics.

University-Level Tutor: For the past 3 years, I have provided academic tutoring in subjects such as Linear Algebra, Ordinary Differential Equations, Real Analysis (Metric Topology), Abstract Algebra, Differential Topology of Curves and Surfaces, and Probability Theory. Additionally, I have tutored students from external universities, including courses like Mathematical Analysis and Analytic Geometry for students at the National and Technical University of Athens.

Author: I am co-authoring a book on the Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces in collaboration with the tutoring center SupM.

Education

Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics: National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, with an average grade of 9.46/10.

Master's Degree in Pure Mathematics: Completed a Master's degree at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, with an average grade of 9.66/10. My thesis, titled "Every module has a flat cover," was supervised by Professor Ioannis Emmanouil.

Volunteering

Since my second year as an undergraduate, I have been volunteering in the Department of Mathematics, contributing to various activities. Together with Tasos Fragkos, Ioannis Oikonomidis, and Vaggelis Katsadonis, I co-organized the "Mathematics Group," a series of weekly events where both undergraduate and graduate students present topics spanning a wide range of mathematical disciplines. These events are held in collaboration with the University of Athens.
